Questions

Which phones does BIB work on?

iPhone today. An Android version is in the works. A web view of races is planned too.

Where do the dates come from?

From each race’s own website and official registration pages. If a date is only an estimate, BIB labels it “Est.” and shows what it is based on. If there is no reliable date, it says “date unknown.”

Do I have to create an account?

You can preview the app first without one. You need a free account to set alerts and save your races.

Does BIB send me marketing or spam?

No. Notifications are the alerts for races you chose to watch. You control them in the app.

How does BIB make money?

From Premium subscriptions. BIB may also earn a small commission when you register through some partner links (such as RunSignup). It never changes the price you pay.

Is BIB connected to the races it lists?

No. BIB is independent and not affiliated with or endorsed by the races, organizers or event brands it shows. Their names belong to them.

A date looks wrong or a race is missing. What do I do?

Tap Report on the race in the app, or write to support@bibregistry.com. Accuracy is the whole point, so we fix it.
