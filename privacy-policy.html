Privacy Policy

BIB is a race registration deadline tracker. This page explains what data the app collects, why, and how you can remove it.

Account

Email address and password, handled by our authentication provider (Supabase). We never see or store your password in plain text.

Profile

Display name, home location — used only to calculate distance to races and offer a default search radius, changeable or clearable any time — timezone, and search radius preference.

Races you watch

Which races and registration paths you've asked to be notified about, and your alert preferences: push on or off, how far in advance to remind you, and quiet hours.

Push notification token

If you enable notifications, we store a token issued by Apple or Google, via Expo's push service, that lets us deliver an alert to your device. It identifies your device for notifications only — it is not an advertising identifier, and we don't use it for anything else.

Race history you enter

Past race results, times, and notes you choose to add. This is entered by you and never inferred or scraped from elsewhere.

Photos

If you photograph a medal or upload one from your library, it's stored privately, associated only with your account. We don't scan, analyze, or share these images.

Reports

If you report a problem with a race listing, we store what you submit — which race, what's wrong, any details you add — so we can fix it.

App usage

We keep a basic record of how the app is used — which screens are opened and when you sign in — tied to your account, so we can see which parts of BIB people actually use and improve them. This stays inside our own systems: we don't share it with advertisers, analytics companies, or anyone else, and it's never used to track you outside the app.

What we don't do

  • We don't run ads and don't share your data with advertisers.
  • We don't sell your data.
  • We don't track you across other apps or websites.

Who else sees it

Supabase hosts our database, authentication, and file storage, and processes data on our behalf under their own security and privacy commitments — they don't independently use your data.

Expo delivers push notifications on our behalf using the token described above.

Data submitted through registration links you tap goes to the race's own registration platform (e.g. RunSignup) — that's between you and them, governed by their privacy policy, not ours.

We do not otherwise share your personal data with third parties.

Your controls

  • Edit or clear your profile information any time in Settings.
  • Unwatch any race, or adjust your alert preferences, any time.
  • Delete your account from within the app (Settings → Delete Account). This permanently removes your profile, watches, results, photos, and device token, and cannot be undone.

Children

BIB is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the update.
