Support

BIB tracks race registration deadlines — lottery windows, general registration opens and closes, qualifier deadlines — so you don't miss the window because you were looking at the wrong week.

Why doesn't a race I watched show a countdown yet?

Not every race has a confirmed date yet. We'd rather show "date not set" than guess wrong on a lottery date. Watched races without a date show up tagged "Awaiting date" and fill in as we confirm them.

How do notifications work?

Enable push notifications in Settings, then watch a race. You'll get a reminder before registration opens, before it closes, and — for lotteries — when the drawing happens and when results post. Adjust how far in advance you're reminded, and set quiet hours, in Settings.

A race's date, link, or status looks wrong. What do I do?

Tap the race, then "Report a problem." Tell us what's wrong and we'll fix it.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Delete Account. This is permanent and removes everything — watches, results, photos — right away.

Is BIB free?

Yes, currently free. If a paid tier launches later, existing functionality you're using won't be taken away without notice.
